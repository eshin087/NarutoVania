## Narrative continuity

1. Team7 encounters Zabuza lakeside. Kakashi confronts him, is deceived and captured.
2. Naruto and Sasuke overcome the water clone. Sasuke throws paired windmill weapons; hidden Naruto reappears and throws one small shuriken. Contact causes recoil and prison release.
3. Kakashi resumes his stance, exchanges Water Dragons with Zabuza and wins with a subsequent dragon. This is an accepted gameplay/cinematic adaptation, not a claim that every finishing detail reproduces the manga literally.
4. Three offscreen senbon strike Zabuza. Haku appears as a hunter-nin, explains once and carries him fully offscreen.
5. At the bridge, Sakura protects Tazuna while Kakashi/Zabuza clash. Sasuke engages Haku; mirrors form around the young fighters.
6. Naruto joins before Sasuke's sacrifice. Sasuke's Sharingan recognition and evasion lead into his interception of needles aimed at Naruto. Naruto approaches after the collapse and awakens Nine-Tails chakra.
7. Naruto defeats Haku. Haku unmasks beside him and fallen Sasuke; Kakashi/Zabuza are fighting farther left.
8. The adult duel escalates to hound restraint and Chidori. Haku intercepts before Kakashi strikes Zabuza; hounds dismiss at contact.
9. Gato's betrayal leads to Zabuza's final stand. Naruto hands his kunai to Zabuza, who attacks with the weapon in his mouth; survivors flee.
10. Kakashi helps Zabuza reach Haku. Carry/lower actions preserve body positions into the snowy resting tableau.

Do not omit narrative beats just because portrait/manga panels were removed. All scenes are in-engine with automatic concise bubbles and scene-local skip.

## Accepted gameplay adaptations and preferences

- Arc-era costumes and character identity; no Sasuke Chidori or Naruto Rasengan.
- Kakashi has Sharingan, hounds and a Chidori ultimate even where the encounter kit condenses chronology.
- Naruto uses clones, a fully clothed adult glamour illusion, Clone Barrage, and awakened Chakra Rush/Fury.
- Sasuke uses Great Fireball, Windmill Shuriken and Sharingan Focus.
- Sakura has protection/counter animation previews; no redundant playable protection fight.
- Signature water/ice barrages expand the spectacle. Two distinct perfect-parried volleys force Haku out; ordinary attacks/guard breaks/ultimates remain alternatives.
- Reflections form progressively, are identical before a tell, disappear during Haku's exit, and reappear only through re-entry.
- Boss HP/damage and defensive windows remain as implemented; rescue550HP and very short duration were explicitly accepted.
- Each fight/retry starts with one ultimate; active combat earns another. Visual extra strikes do not add damage.
- Stable body scale, quiet idle and approved older runs. Naruto/Sasuke are visually5% smaller than their older reference; collision size unchanged.
- No yellow anticipation arcs, white occupied-mirror halo, red floor blocks or manga portrait interruptions.
- Quiet licensed decoded audio and muted placeholder voices. Prior V23 receipt did not include subjective listening or physical-controller verification.
