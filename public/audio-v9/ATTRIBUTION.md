# Combat audio v9 credits and reuse terms

These recordings are integrated into the Naruto fan game. They are not offered as a general sound library. Every final clip lists its original recording, selection time, transformations, and layer gains in `manifest.json`.

## Taira Komori

Sound effects by **Taira Komori**, [TK'S Free Sound FX](https://taira-komori.net/freesounden.html). Original recordings selected from the [Fighting](https://taira-komori.net/attack01.html), [Samurai / Ninja](https://taira-komori.net/jidaigeki01en.html), [Magic / Fantasy](https://taira-komori.net/magic01en.html), and [Nature](https://taira-komori.net/nature01en.html) pages.

The creator's terms, checked 2026-09-06, allow free commercial and noncommercial project use. The terms prohibit redistribution as individual sound effects or a sound library, selling/relicensing the sounds, claiming authorship, and hotlinking individual sound files. These recordings were edited and layered for this game. This is a custom project-use license, **not CC0**. Keep the credits with the game and do not publish this staging folder as a downloadable sound pack.

## HaelDB

**Male Grunt/Yelling sounds** by **HaelDB**, [source page](https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The page offers CC0 alongside OGA-BY 3.0; this pack uses the CC0 option. Verified 2026-09-06. The source describes four different male vocalists. Efforts here use existing recorded performances with trimmed envelopes, EQ, and modest rate changes; no actor voice was cloned or imitated.
